I am a Ph.D. candidate in the College of Media and International Culture at Zhejiang University, advised by Dr. Yingru Ji, and currently a visiting student at the Annenberg School for Communication, University of Southern California, advised by Dr. Angel Hsing-Chi Hwang.

I hold an MSc with Distinction in Media and Communications (Data and Society) from the London School of Economics and Political Science, where I was advised by Dr. Ellen Helsper, and a BA in Broadcasting and Television from Zhejiang University.

My research centers on three interconnected areas:

  • Human–AI interaction — social experience and social dynamics, with a focus on attributional processes.
  • AI-mediated communication — its influence on interpersonal communication.
  • AI for organizations — how organizations and employees respond to the opportunities and challenges brought by AI.

I am always happy to connect with fellow researchers and practitioners. If any of my research interests resonate with your work, I would welcome the opportunity to discuss ideas and explore potential collaborations.
